# Rough Example: Squad Layout

**Proposed (2026-10-23):** an enemy squad is a named list of ship designs with counts per tier. The ship designs
(`ship-suggestions.md`) carry everything else: systems, weapons and behaviour by state. Formation (GDD 3H),
behaviour (race profiles), rewards (GDD 8I) and difficulty (bands) are defined elsewhere and not repeated here.

**Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-23):** two optional extras: count ranges (e.g. 1–2), so the same squad doesn't
always look identical, and one line saying where the squad shows up. Mercenaries (GDD 7G) are just another line.

## Example: Terran Pirate Squad

Rough example only. The Pirate Hijacker exists in `ship-suggestions.md` (a mid-tier, band 5 design); the other ship names are
placeholders for ships not designed yet.

**Shows up in:** doomed or struggling Terran clusters, and borders (including Mecha border clusters, where
criminals hide).

| Tier | Ships |
|---|---|
| Low (bands 1–3) | 1 × [light raider] · 0–1 × [pirate scout] |
| Mid (bands 4–6) | 1–2 × Pirate Hijacker · 1 × [light raider] · 0–1 × [mercenary, any race that hires out] |
| High (bands 7–8) | 1–2 × [heavy pirate ship] · 2 × Pirate Hijacker Mk II · 1 × [light raider] Mk II |
