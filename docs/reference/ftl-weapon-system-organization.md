# Potential FTL Weapon and System Organization

A first sort of FTL's weapons, systems and drones by which Infall race they might suit, based on the race profiles
(`docs/race-profiles.md`). Nothing here is decided; it's a starting point for giving each race its own versions.
Names are FTL's; Infall versions would get their own names. Boss-only and enemy-only weapons are left out. Some
items fit more than one race and are listed under each.

## Common to all races

Systems every race is expected to have in some form: Shields, Engines, Oxygen (Life Support), Weapons, Piloting,
Sensors, Doors, Medbay.

## Terrans (Gatos)

Federation-like, balanced, clean tech; unlimited-power AZC with conduits.

- **Weapons:** Dual Shot Laser, Burst Laser I / II / III, Heavy Laser I / II, Laser Charger I / II, Leto
  Missiles, Artemis Missiles, Hermes Missile, Pegasus Missile, Small Bomb
- **Systems:** Artillery (Federation-style; Flak Artillery, Artillery Beam), Crew Teleporter
- **Drone-like:** System Repair Drone

## Azurethea

Near-pacifist, biomechanical; disable rather than destroy; single-room shots; spores that disable, stun and
(idea) mind control; high evasion.

- **Weapons:** Mini Beam, Pike Beam (single energy beams), Leto / Hermes Missiles (stinger missiles), Ion Blast,
  Ion Stunner (as spore equivalents), Stun Bomb, Crystal Lockdown Bomb, Repair Burst, Healing Burst
- **Systems:** Mind Control (via spores, idea)
- **Drone-like:** System Repair Drone (biomechanical repair)

## Mecha

Hull destruction, high damage, little finesse; crew-powered ships; EVB Dock.

- **Weapons:** Hull Smasher Laser I / II, Hull Beam, Hull Missile, Breach Missiles, Breach Bomb I / II, Halberd
  Beam, Glaive Beam, Heavy Laser II
- **Systems:** EVB Dock (replaces the Drone Control system)
- **Drone-like, as EVB battlesuits:** Combat Drone I / II, Anti-Ship Beam Drone I / II, Anti-Ship Missile Drone,
  Boarding Drone. (Not the Anti-Personnel Drone: a battlesuit fighting boarders would just be boarding its own
  ship; Mecha crew already fill that role.)

## Tentauri

Shields and defence specialists; adapt their weapons as needed; few rooms with many systems.

- **Weapons:** Defense Laser Mark I, Burst Laser I / II, Pike Beam, Halberd Beam, Leto / Artemis Missiles
- **Systems:** Zoltan-like shield (extra shield layer), Backup Battery
- **Drone-like:** Defense Drone I / II, Anti-Combat Drone, Shield Overcharger

## Senti-GPH

Advanced beams, good sensors, hacking, shield-penetrating weapons; not likely missiles; don't usually use ion.

- **Weapons:** Mini Beam, Pike Beam, Halberd Beam, Glaive Beam, Fire Beam, Heavy Pierce Laser, Crystal Burst I /
  II and Heavy Crystal I / II (shield-piercing)
- **Systems:** Hacking, AAA (replaces the Crew Teleporter for their own crew), advanced Sensors
- **Drone-like:** Hacking Drone

## Lykario

Quick-attack raiders; basic, stolen non-biomechanical tech; large or multiple crew teleporter rooms.

- **Weapons:** Dual Shot Laser, Burst Laser I, Chain Burst Laser, Chain Vulcan, Flak Gun I, Ion Blast (to disable
  before boarding), Stun Bomb, Small Bomb
- **Systems:** Crew Teleporter (large or multiple), Cloaking (ambush hunters; suggestion, since cloaking has no
  race yet)
- **Drone-like:** Anti-Personnel Drone, Boarding Drone (from stolen tech)

## Malphen

Infection that spreads; fleshy missiles and flak to get through shields; high HP; clone bay.

- **Weapons:** Flak Gun I / II, Adv. Flak Gun, Swarm Missiles, Breach Missiles, Fire Bomb and Fire Beam (spread
  like infection), Anti-Bio Beam
- **Systems:** Clone Bay
- **Drone-like, as mutations/spawn:** Boarding Drone (Malphen launching themselves, one-way), Anti-Ship Fire
  Drone (infection spreader), Hull Repair Drone (regrowth)

## Whispwraith (non-core)

Ion weapons; 2 bars of power per Whispwraith; self-teleporting crew.

- **Weapons:** Ion Blast I / II, Heavy Ion, Ion Charger, Chain Ion, Ion Stunner, Ion Bomb
- **Drone-like:** Ion Intruder Drone, Anti-Ship Ion Drone

## Not yet placed

- **Drone Control** as a standard system: drones may be removed; their roles are spread above as EVB
  battlesuits (Mecha), mutations/spawn (Malphen) and defence drones (Tentauri).
