# Potential FTL Weapon and System Organization

A first sort of FTL's weapons, systems and drones by which Infall race they might suit, based on what each item
actually does (stats from `ftl-baseline.md`) and the race profiles (`docs/race-profiles.md`). Nothing here is
decided. Names are FTL's; Infall versions would get their own names. Boss-only and enemy-only weapons are left
out.

## What the FTL weapons do

| Group | Weapons | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Basic lasers | Defense Laser Mark I (a "very weak laser" despite the name: 1 shot, 1 damage), Dual Shot, Burst Laser I / II / III (2 / 3 / 5 shots) | Projectiles, 1 damage per shot; each shot can be blocked by a shield layer. General-purpose damage. |
| Heavy lasers | Heavy Laser I / II, Heavy Pierce Laser | 2 damage per shot, 30% fire and breach chance; Heavy Pierce also pierces 1 shield layer. |
| Hull Smashers | Hull Smasher I / II | 1 damage per shot, double damage to rooms without systems; built to maximize hull damage. |
| Chargers / chain | Laser Charger I / II, Chain Burst Laser, Chain Vulcan | Chargers store extra shots; chain weapons fire faster after each volley. |
| Ion | Ion Blast I / II, Heavy Ion, Ion Charger, Chain Ion, Ion Stunner | No hull damage; ion drains shields and disables systems temporarily. Ion Stunner also stuns crew. |
| Beams | Mini, Pike, Halberd, Glaive | Sweep a line across several rooms, damaging hull and systems in each; reduced by shields, so they need shields down first. |
| Special beams | Fire Beam (starts fires, no hull damage), Anti-Bio Beam (no physical damage; heavy damage to crew), Hull Beam (double damage in systemless rooms) | |
| Flak | Flak Gun I / II, Adv. Flak Gun | A spread of 3 or 7 projectiles; strips shield layers. |
| Missiles | Leto (1 dmg), Artemis (2), Hermes (3), Pegasus (2 x 2), Breach Missiles (4, 80% breach), Hull Missile (2, double in systemless rooms), Swarm Missiles (charged) | Ignore shields; use a missile each; can be shot down by defence drones. |
| Crystal | Crystal Burst I / II, Heavy Crystal I / II | Pierce 1 shield layer; can lock down the room hit. |
| Bombs | Small Bomb (system damage), Breach Bomb I / II, Fire Bomb, Ion Bomb, Stun Bomb, Crystal Lockdown Bomb (seals a room, no damage), Healing Burst (heals friendly crew in a room), Repair Burst (fully repairs a system) | Teleport into a room, ignoring shields; use a missile each. Healing and Repair Burst can target your own ship. |

## Common to all races

Systems every race is expected to have in some form: Shields, Engines, Oxygen (Life Support), Weapons, Piloting,
Sensors, Doors, Medbay. General-purpose basic lasers and basic missiles also suit any race.

## Terrans (GATOS)

Federation-like, balanced, clean tech; unlimited-power AZC with conduits.

- **Weapons:** the FTL baseline mix: Burst Laser I / II / III, Dual Shot, Heavy Laser I / II, Laser Charger I /
  II, Leto / Artemis / Hermes / Pegasus Missiles, Small Bomb
- **Systems:** Crew Teleporter
- **Drone-like:** System Repair Drone

## Azurethea

Near-pacifist; disable rather than destroy; single-system targeting shots; spores that disable systems, stun crew
and (idea) mind control, with no breach or fire; high evasion.

- **Weapons:** Ion Blast I / II, Heavy Ion, Ion Charger (disable without damage, closest to spores), Ion Stunner and
  Stun Bomb (stun crew), Ion Bomb, Crystal Lockdown Bomb (traps crew without harming them), Leto / Artemis Missiles
  (single-room "stinger" shots), Defense Laser Mark I (a single-shot laser; fits their "single-shot energy" weapon
  because it fires one shot at one room, not because of its name), Healing Burst and Repair Burst (support, can
  target their own ship)
- **Systems:** Mind Control (via spores, idea)
- **Drone-like:** System Repair Drone

## Mecha

Hull destruction, high damage, little finesse; crew-powered ships; EVB Dock.

- **Weapons:** Hull Smasher I / II, Hull Beam, Hull Missile (hull damage), Breach Missiles, Breach Bomb I / II,
  Heavy Laser I / II, Hermes Missile, Halberd and Glaive Beams (heavy damage across rooms)
- **Systems:** EVB Dock (replaces the Drone Control system)
- **Drone-like, as EVB battlesuits:** Combat Drone I / II, Anti-Ship Beam Drone I / II, Anti-Ship Missile Drone,
  Boarding Drone. (Not the Anti-Personnel Drone: a battlesuit fighting boarders would just be boarding its own
  ship; Mecha crew already fill that role.)

## Tentauri

Shields and defence specialists; weapons are standard lasers, beams or missiles as needed.

- **Weapons:** standard choices: Burst Laser I / II, Pike / Halberd Beam, Artemis Missiles
- **Systems:** Zoltan-like shield (extra shield layer), Backup Battery
- **Drone-like:** Defense Drone I / II (shoot down missiles and drones), Anti-Combat Drone, Shield Overcharger

## Senti-GPH

Advanced beams, good sensors, hacking, shield-penetrating weapons; not likely missiles; don't usually use ion.

- **Weapons:** Pike, Halberd and Glaive Beams; Anti-Bio Beam (kills crew while leaving the ship intact, fitting
  their doomed state); Fire Beam (they are immune to fire); Heavy Pierce Laser, Crystal Burst I / II and Heavy
  Crystal I / II (pierce shields, no missiles needed)
- **Systems:** Hacking, AAA, advanced Sensors
- **Drone-like:** Hacking Drone

## Lykario

Quick-attack raiders; basic stolen tech; large or multiple crew teleporter rooms; cloaking.

- **Weapons:** Chain Burst Laser and Chain Vulcan (speed up the longer they fire), Dual Shot, Burst Laser I, Ion
  Blast (knock out shields or weapons before boarding), Stun Bomb (stun crew before boarding), Healing Burst (heal
  their boarders on the enemy ship)
- **Systems:** Crew Teleporter (large or multiple), Cloaking
- **Drone-like:** Anti-Personnel Drone (stolen; defends their own ship against counter-boarders)

## Malphen

Infection that spreads; fleshy missiles and flak to get through shields; high HP; clone bay.

- **Weapons:** Flak Gun I / II, Adv. Flak Gun (strip shields), Leto / Artemis Missiles and Swarm Missiles (fleshy
  missiles), Fire Bomb and Fire Beam (fire spreads room to room, the closest FTL analog to infection), Breach
  Missiles, Anti-Bio Beam
- **Systems:** Clone Bay; a mutation system (idea) that lets Malphen crew swap their specialty, e.g.
  anti-personnel, repair, fire, launch
- **Drone-like, as mutations/spawn:** Boarding Drone (Malphen launching themselves through an airlock or launching
  bay, via a launch mutation), Anti-Ship Fire Drone (infection spreader), Hull Repair Drone (regrowth)

## Whispwraith (non-core)

Ion weapons; 2 bars of power per Whispwraith; self-teleporting crew.

- **Weapons:** Ion Blast I / II, Heavy Ion, Ion Charger, Chain Ion, Ion Stunner, Ion Bomb
- **Drone-like:** Ion Intruder Drone, Anti-Ship Ion Drone

## Not yet placed

- **Artillery:** if used, either a specialized weapon different for each race, maybe on their highest-tier ships,
  or given to the Mecha.
- **Drone Control** as a standard system: drones may be removed; their roles are spread above as EVB
  battlesuits (Mecha), mutations/spawn (Malphen) and defence drones (Tentauri).
