# Economy Findings

Findings are numbered (F1, F2…) so they can be answered by number. Everything suggested here is
Ai-Generated-Proposal until heromedel reviews it; decisions he gives are recorded with their tag.

## Decisions heard

- **Decided (2026-10-23):** A jump costs 1.21 Mawjig for the whole squad, not per ship. Charging per ship would only
  make big squads more expensive, and FTL's balance is built for one ship. 1.21 is fixed, not open to tuning: it
  comes from the 1.21 jigawatts.
- **Proposed (2026-10-23):** The economy's target is FTL's buying power per sector, with Infall's differences
  (squad growth, the shared pool) set against it. The long-term aim: a squad playing smart can, through fights and
  events, survive and upgrade or gain ships enough to have a chance of winning, and that chance is something to tune.

- **Likely (2026-10-23):** band 1 starts near FTL, but later bands may need rewards to rise faster than FTL's to pay

## F2. A jump takes a larger share of a reward than FTL's fuel

A jump is 1.21 Mawjig, about 5 scrap. FTL's fuel costs about 3 scrap in a store (approximate, `ftl-baseline.md`).
Against a band 1 fight, the jump takes about 35% of the reward (1.21 ÷ 3.42); FTL's fuel takes about 21% of a
sector 1 reward, and FTL's rewards also hand out fuel. So Infall's travel is about 1.7× as tight as FTL's in band 1.
If F1 is taken, the share falls each band (about 9% by band 8), as it does in FTL.

No change suggested yet: tighter travel may be wanted, since the collapse is chasing the squad. Worth checking in
the simulation once fights per band are known.

**heromedel (2026-10-23), Proposed:** the fix is the one in F1: rewards need to be higher eventually. Losing FTL's
fuel, missile and drone-part rewards did lose some reward flavour, but Infall may offer whole ships and systems more
often, and a lot more crew, eventually.

## F3. Missiles are about 29× cheaper to fire than in FTL

An Infall missile shot is 0.05 Mawjig, about 0.21 scrap. An FTL missile costs about 6 scrap in a store. Lasers go
from free to 0.02. A whole early fight's shots (0.4–0.6) cost under half a jump and about 15% of the reward.

In FTL, missile scarcity is a real choice: fire it now or save it for a harder ship. In Infall that choice mostly
disappears: a shield-ignoring missile costs barely more to fire than a laser, and weapon prices no longer carry
FTL's ammo discount (`weapon-budget-table.md`), so missile weapons are dearer to buy but nearly free to use. The
same is true of drones: an FTL drone part is about 8 scrap per deployment.

**heromedel (2026-10-23):** fine for now. Squads fire a lot more: 6 ships with 2–4 weapons each adds up. Whether
shot costs are felt is not a question for now; recheck once squad-sized fights can be simulated.

## F4. Squad growth: the economy holds only if rewards are paid per enemy ship

An estimate, not a simulation: there are no Infall ships yet, so FTL's numbers stand in for them. No losses.

**Assumptions** (heromedel's: the squad grows by one ship every two bands, no losses; the rest are mine):

| | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Squad size by band | 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 | heromedel |
| Jumps per band | 10 | GDD 2O |
| Fights per band | 7 | Calibrated: with 3 events, FTL's income is about 300 scrap by the end of sector 2, as players report |
| Other events per band | 3, each paying FTL's Low reward | Same calibration |
| Reward | Ship destroyed, from the cost table | Crew kills would pay more |
| Hull lost per ship per fight | 2 (bands 1–3), 3 (4–6), 4 (7–8) | Judgment |
| Ammo per ship per fight | 0.64 (bands 1–2), 0.96 (3–5), 1.28 (6–8) | The cost table's typical fight, × 1.5 and × 2 as ships gain weapons |
| FTL ship for comparison | Same fights; fuel 3 scrap a jump, 1 missile a fight at 6 scrap | `ftl-baseline.md` |

**Result:** Mawjig left over for upgrades in each band, per ship, compared with an FTL ship:

| Band | Ships | Reward per fight: per ship | ÷ FTL | Reward per enemy ship: per ship | ÷ FTL | FTL one ship |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1 | 1 | 6.81 | 1.14 | 6.81 | 1.14 | 5.97 |
| 2 | 1 | 18.80 | 1.05 | 18.80 | 1.05 | 17.96 |
| 3 | 2 | 7.87 | 0.26 | 29.61 | 0.97 | 30.58 |
| 4 | 2 | 5.47 | 0.16 | 31.97 | 0.93 | 34.39 |
| 5 | 3 | 0.51 | 0.01 | 42.55 | 0.91 | 47.01 |
| 6 | 3 | 2.40 | 0.04 | 51.03 | 0.86 | 59.43 |
| 7 | 4 | −13.09 | −0.22 | 48.76 | 0.82 | 59.66 |
| 8 | 4 | −9.95 | −0.14 | 59.41 | 0.82 | 72.21 |

"Per enemy ship" assumes the enemy squad is as big as the player's.

1. **If a fight pays one reward however many ships it has,** a squad starves from band 3. Repairs and ammo grow with
   every ship, but income doesn't; by band 7 the squad loses Mawjig on every fight.
2. **If each destroyed enemy ship pays its own reward** (each wreck is matter to convert, GDD 8C), each ship gets
   about 80–100% of an FTL ship's upgrade money. The shared jump (6C) helps a bigger squad; the extra ammo hurts.
3. **This fits the Likely point in the cost table:** the gap opens late (82% in bands 7–8) and is mild, so rewards
   rising about 20% faster than FTL's in bands 7–8 would close it.
4. **Not counted:** the price of gaining a ship (free from events, or bought?) and bringing it up to the squad's
   level. Each bought ship would cost on top of the upgrade money above.

**Likely (2026-10-23):** fight rewards are paid per enemy ship destroyed or left without crew, using the band
table. The size of enemy squads compared with the player's then becomes the economy's main dial: smaller enemy
squads mean a poorer run.

## F5. Answers to Ship Designs' pricing questions (2026-10-23)

Ship Designs sent 13 questions through heromedel. His answers are tagged from his words; the rest are mine
(Ai-Generated-Proposal), checked against FTL's data where it has the number.

**Rules to settle**

1. **Wait gain — Likely (heromedel, 2026-10-23):** about five Waits buy one jump (1.21). Mawjig shows two decimals
   and never ends in a zero, so 0.242 can't be shown: **0.25 per Wait** (five give 1.25); 0.24 would need six (1.20
   is short). GDD 6B and the cost table updated.
2. **Battlesuit launch — heromedel:** priced by its shots, since that is what it is worth as a weapon or drone. A
   launch costs its sortie's shots (0.21 for the 10-shot laser suit); the "new suit every launch" is lore inside
   that price. **Recycling refund — Proposed (heromedel, 2026-10-23): keep it.** It nets to nothing, but getting
   something back feels good. **Raised to 0.02 (heromedel, 2026-10-23):** rounder, a small payoff, and it makes up
   for lost suits and the hassle. A launch stays **0.22**; a suit that comes home gives **0.02** back, so a returned
   suit costs 0.20 and has a slight chance of being worth it, and a suit destroyed in battle gives nothing back.

   **Note — heromedel (2026-10-23):** battlesuits come out much cheaper than FTL's drones (about 8 scrap, 1.94 Mawjig,
   per deployment). The risk of losing the crew member inside probably more than makes up for it; if suits still
   look too cheap in play, their price is the thing to change. Drones likewise pay per shot and stay out until the battle ends; interior drones last.
3. **Mecha learning and the Cybernetic Servicing Bay** (heromedel's rule: half the XP to learn, the XP saved is
   paid at 0.01 Mawjig a point). FTL's XP per skill level (FTL wiki, from memory, not in the data files): Piloting
   15, Engines 15, Shields 55, Weapons 65, Repair 18, Combat 8; mastery is twice that.

   | Skill | Mecha XP per level | Paid per level |
   |---|:-:|:-:|
   | Piloting | 7.5 | 0.08 |
   | Engines | 7.5 | 0.08 |
   | Shields | 27.5 | 0.28 |
   | Weapons | 32.5 | 0.33 |
   | Repair | 9 | 0.09 |
   | Combat | 4 | 0.04 |

   If a mech upgrade level opens one skill level for every skill at once (Ai-Generated-Proposal): **0.88 per mech
   level** (1.76 for both). Under a jump, as heromedel wanted. At 0.01 per two points it would be 0.44, which hardly
   registers, so 0.01 a point is suggested. heromedel's placeholder of about 0.25 is the same size as one skill level
   here. **heromedel (2026-10-23):** to be recalculated once skill gain is normalized.

   **Superseded by GDD 7H (skills normalized: 100 XP to level 1, 150 more to level 2; Mecha earn XP at 2×).**
   **Mech upgrade price — Proposed (heromedel, 2026-10-23):** per individual skill, **0.25** for level 1 and
   **0.37** for level 2, i.e. 0.005 Mawjig per XP point a Mecha saves (50 and 75 points). Mastering one skill costs
   0.62; all six, 3.72.

   **Second power bar — heromedel (2026-10-23):** it isn't buying a whole Zoltan, it is a reactor bar's worth of
   portable energy, so a one-time upgrade worth 30–35 scrap.

   **Mecha power cap — Proposed (heromedel, 2026-10-23):** raise the cap from 1–2 bars to 3 or 4, so a highly
   upgraded Mecha can power a mixed weapon bay alone. Bar 1 comes with hiring (probably more than a Zoltan); bars 2,
   3 and 4 are one-time upgrades of 25, 30 and 35 scrap: **6.05, 7.26, 8.47** (21.78 for all three).
4. **Volley cost rounding** (Ai-Generated-Proposal): a weapon's per-shot cost is rounded to hundredths when the
   weapon is built (half up, at least 0.01), then the no-trailing-zero rule. A volley is per-shot × shots; a volley
   landing on x.x0 adds 0.01. The squad's running total can show any amount. So 3 × 0.021 is 3 × 0.02 = 0.06; the
   DS-ST Hull Laser's 0.036 per shot becomes 0.04, and its volley 0.08 (not 0.07).

**Pricing rules to check**

5. **Hull bonus — checks out.** The model without a hull part misses FTL's Hull Smasher I by +1.30 s and II by
   +0.07 s per shot (average 0.7), and their prices by 1.05 and 0.53 of the damage-rate term (average about 0.8).
   +0.7 s and 0.75 are the averages; the two lasers disagree, so it stays a judgment.
6. **Mecha self-power — checks out.** Bars priced as reactor bars across the ship; integrated-crew bars are free

7. **Spores and stun — checks out.** Ion Stunner over Ion Blast: +5 scrap (1.21) and +2 s, as in the tables.

9. **Conduit damage — no change** (Proposed).

    (2026-10-23):** price it from the Zoltan Shield augment plus the Shield Overcharger drone, and maybe a third of a

    80 + 60 + 20 (a third of Drone Control's 60) = 160 scrap = **38.72** (now 19.36; 33.88 without the Drone

    Capacity 5 is still unverified: the data's only number for the Zoltan Shield is a `value` of 2.
11. **Crew prices and drone per-use — reviewed.** Crew prices hold, except **Tentauri**: running up to four systems

**Balance checks**

12. **Fight assumptions — plausible.** The Outrider's 15% is FTL-like (engines 2, manned pilot). The Mecha frames
    have a manned pilot, so 10% rather than 5% (engines 1 plus piloting). The Outrider's 1.56 left against one band 1
    enemy matches the cost table's 1.57.
13. **Squad sizes per band.** Rewards are paid per enemy ship (8I), so two light ships pay twice what one standard

    - Enemy squads track the player's expected squad (F4: 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4), one ship larger late, where

      | Band | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
      |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
      | Enemy ships | 1 | 1–2 | 2 | 2–3 | 3 | 3–4 | 4–5 | 4–6 |

      One more enemy ship in bands 7–8 also pays the roughly 20% that F4 found missing there.

      **heromedel (2026-10-23):** it depends on ship strength: enemy loadouts can be deliberately weaker than the
      player's, to leave room in encounter and squad design. So the count of ships is not the measure.

**Rewards from ship value (heromedel, 2026-10-23)**

- A wreck pays **2.5% of the ship's value × a band multiplier**, plus any story or event rewards. Ship value makes
  more sense than a flat amount per band. A Light Attack Frame (131.72) pays 3.29 in band 1, close to the old 3.42.
- Your own lost ship, if salvage is allowed and the event or encounter lets you: **2.5% of its value, no multiplier**,
  as it isn't a reward. (GDD 1I said about a quarter; 2.5% replaces it, if agreed.)
- Infall has no drones (ideas: drones are scrapped). An on-board anti-personnel suit, and any repair or
  anti-personnel mutation, could pay for its maximum shots at launch and get the unused shots back when it returns
  (heromedel, 2026-10-23, an idea).
    - Yes, band 1 can field a light pair, as an occasional richer fight (about 1 in 3 band 1 fights at most), not the
      norm; otherwise band 1 income rises by up to 70%.
    - For heromedel, not settled: a wreck's reward could scale with the ship's size (hull 7 against a band standard of
      about 10 pays 0.7×), which makes light pairs pay like one ship. That would change 8I, so it is his call.

## F6. Enemy squads stocked from a value budget (2026-10-23)

- **Proposed (heromedel, 2026-10-23):** a wreck pays from the ship's value, with only a small band adjustment on
  top, e.g. up to about ×1.3, so the value does the main work and late rewards don't balloon.
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal:** each band has a squad value budget, worked backwards from the reward a fight should
  pay: one band reward (cost table) per ship the player is expected to have (F4), ÷ (2.5% × band adjustment).
- **Band adjustment — Proposed (heromedel, 2026-10-23):** smooth, ×1.(band − 1): ×1.0 in band 1 up to ×1.7 in band 8.
  It multiplies the reward only; a ship's value never changes. Band 1 stays as calibrated (×1.0), and a Light Attack
  Frame met in band 8 still pays 5.60, about 6 hull points of repair: worth fighting, not worth farming.
