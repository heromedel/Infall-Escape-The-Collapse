# Economy Findings

Findings are numbered (F1, F2…) so they can be answered by number. Everything suggested here is
Ai-Generated-Proposal until heromedel reviews it; decisions he gives are recorded with their tag.

## Decisions heard

- **Decided (2026-10-23):** A jump costs 1.21 Mawjig for the whole squad, not per ship. Charging per ship would only
  make big squads more expensive, and FTL's balance is built for one ship. 1.21 is fixed, not open to tuning: it
  comes from the 1.21 jigawatts.
- **Proposed (2026-10-23):** The economy's target is FTL's buying power per sector, with Infall's differences
  (squad growth, the shared pool) set against it. The long-term aim: a squad playing smart can, through fights and
  events, survive and upgrade or gain ships enough to have a chance of winning, and that chance is something to tune.

The draft's 3.42 matches FTL's sector 1 almost exactly (3.41), but FTL's rewards rise every sector (about +1.4

**Suggestion (Ai-Generated-Proposal):** rewards follow FTL's per-sector curve, one step per band, × 0.242, with the
no-trailing-zero rule. Killing the crew pays more than destroying the ship, as in FTL; a ship left intact has more
matter to convert into Mawjig, which fits the wreckage fiction (GDD 8C).

**heromedel (2026-10-23), Proposed:** following FTL more closely is smarter for now. Eventually the game may need
more chances for rewards, or more reward in general. The per-band rewards are averages; single fights vary.

- **Decided (2026-10-23):** killing the crew pays more than destroying the ship, as in FTL.
- **Likely (2026-10-23):** band 1 starts near FTL, but later bands may need rewards to rise faster than FTL's to pay
  for a bigger squad's costs, unless the run allows, or ends up with, more time for grinding.

## F2. A jump takes a larger share of a reward than FTL's fuel

A jump is 1.21 Mawjig, about 5 scrap. FTL's fuel costs about 3 scrap in a store (approximate, `ftl-baseline.md`).
Against a band 1 fight, the jump takes about 35% of the reward (1.21 ÷ 3.42); FTL's fuel takes about 21% of a
sector 1 reward, and FTL's rewards also hand out fuel. So Infall's travel is about 1.7× as tight as FTL's in band 1.
If F1 is taken, the share falls each band (about 9% by band 8), as it does in FTL.

No change suggested yet: tighter travel may be wanted, since the collapse is chasing the squad. Worth checking in
the simulation once fights per band are known.

**heromedel (2026-10-23), Proposed:** the fix is the one in F1: rewards need to be higher eventually. Losing FTL's
fuel, missile and drone-part rewards did lose some reward flavour, but Infall may offer whole ships and systems more
often, and a lot more crew, eventually.

## F3. Missiles are about 29× cheaper to fire than in FTL

An Infall missile shot is 0.05 Mawjig, about 0.21 scrap. An FTL missile costs about 6 scrap in a store. Lasers go
from free to 0.02. A whole early fight's shots (0.4–0.6) cost under half a jump and about 15% of the reward.

In FTL, missile scarcity is a real choice: fire it now or save it for a harder ship. In Infall that choice mostly
disappears: a shield-ignoring missile costs barely more to fire than a laser, and weapon prices no longer carry
FTL's ammo discount (`weapon-budget-table.md`), so missile weapons are dearer to buy but nearly free to use. The
same is true of drones: an FTL drone part is about 8 scrap per deployment.

**heromedel (2026-10-23):** fine for now. Squads fire a lot more: 6 ships with 2–4 weapons each adds up. Whether
shot costs are felt is not a question for now; recheck once squad-sized fights can be simulated.

## F4. Squad growth: the economy holds only if rewards are paid per enemy ship

An estimate, not a simulation: there are no Infall ships yet, so FTL's numbers stand in for them. No losses.

**Assumptions** (heromedel's: the squad grows by one ship every two bands, no losses; the rest are mine):

| | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Squad size by band | 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 | heromedel |
| Jumps per band | 10 | GDD 2O |
| Fights per band | 7 | Calibrated: with 3 events, FTL's income is about 300 scrap by the end of sector 2, as players report |
| Other events per band | 3, each paying FTL's Low reward | Same calibration |
| Reward | Ship destroyed, from the cost table | Crew kills would pay more |
| Hull lost per ship per fight | 2 (bands 1–3), 3 (4–6), 4 (7–8) | Judgment |
| Ammo per ship per fight | 0.64 (bands 1–2), 0.96 (3–5), 1.28 (6–8) | The cost table's typical fight, × 1.5 and × 2 as ships gain weapons |
| FTL ship for comparison | Same fights; fuel 3 scrap a jump, 1 missile a fight at 6 scrap | `ftl-baseline.md` |

**Result:** Mawjig left over for upgrades in each band, per ship, compared with an FTL ship:

| Band | Ships | Reward per fight: per ship | ÷ FTL | Reward per enemy ship: per ship | ÷ FTL | FTL one ship |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1 | 1 | 6.81 | 1.14 | 6.81 | 1.14 | 5.97 |
| 2 | 1 | 18.80 | 1.05 | 18.80 | 1.05 | 17.96 |
| 3 | 2 | 7.87 | 0.26 | 29.61 | 0.97 | 30.58 |
| 4 | 2 | 5.47 | 0.16 | 31.97 | 0.93 | 34.39 |
| 5 | 3 | 0.51 | 0.01 | 42.55 | 0.91 | 47.01 |
| 6 | 3 | 2.40 | 0.04 | 51.03 | 0.86 | 59.43 |
| 7 | 4 | −13.09 | −0.22 | 48.76 | 0.82 | 59.66 |
| 8 | 4 | −9.95 | −0.14 | 59.41 | 0.82 | 72.21 |

"Per enemy ship" assumes the enemy squad is as big as the player's.

1. **If a fight pays one reward however many ships it has,** a squad starves from band 3. Repairs and ammo grow with
   every ship, but income doesn't; by band 7 the squad loses Mawjig on every fight.
2. **If each destroyed enemy ship pays its own reward** (each wreck is matter to convert, GDD 8C), each ship gets
   about 80–100% of an FTL ship's upgrade money. The shared jump (6C) helps a bigger squad; the extra ammo hurts.
3. **This fits the Likely point in the cost table:** the gap opens late (82% in bands 7–8) and is mild, so rewards
   rising about 20% faster than FTL's in bands 7–8 would close it.
4. **Not counted:** the price of gaining a ship (free from events, or bought?) and bringing it up to the squad's
   level. Each bought ship would cost on top of the upgrade money above.

**Suggestion (Ai-Generated-Proposal):** fight rewards are paid per enemy ship destroyed or crewless, using the band
table. The size of enemy squads compared with the player's then becomes the economy's main dial: smaller enemy
squads mean a poorer run.
