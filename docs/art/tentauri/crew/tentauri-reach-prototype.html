Tentauri reach prototype

The Tentauri types at its own console and grows a pair of short tentacles out to up to three more consoles in the same room. The two long tentacles always hang. Tentacles are drawn live at sprite resolution, so any layout works.

Click near the edge of a tile to put a console on that wall. Clicking a console you already placed connects or disconnects it, and a disconnected tentacle pulls back in. With Place Tentauri, the edge you click becomes its own console.

Layout

Edit the numbers and apply. Columns and rows count from 0 at the top left, and the direction is the wall the console sits on.

Line format

room 5 3
width and height in tiles
tentauri 2 0 up
column, row, and the wall its own console is on
console 1 0 up
a connected console
console 4 2 right off
placed but not connected

Only the first three connected consoles get tentacles: two per console uses all eight short tentacles.
