# Tentauri Defense Laser

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Status:** accepted by Ship Designs, with the Tentauri scout. Weapon design: `docs/ship-suggestions.md`, "Tentauri
Defense Laser".

![Defense Laser at 4x](defense-laser-4x.png)

A segmented barrel: three soft nacre segments narrowing to a glowing emitter tip, on a small dark teal pad. Drawn
top-down pointing up, centred on its mount point at (0, 0). Used twice on the Tentauri scout.

**Second pass (2026-10-25):** heromedel found the first one a bit off. Against the genre's weapon sprites at the same
scale it was the right length but too wide, too simple and symmetric, pale enough to wash out on the hull, and its
ringed mouth read as an eye, so the whole thing looked like a lighthouse. Four new versions, narrower and longer with a
glowing tip and darker teal and lavender accents (`defense-laser-options-2.png`): sac and barrel, paired fronds, swept
siphon, and segmented, picked by heromedel because a plain jointed barrel fits other races' ships too.

**First pass (2026-10-23):** a siphon emitter, a smooth tapered nacre tube on a soft seat with a ringed mouth and a
glowing lens, picked from three styles drawn from the race's anatomy (`defense-laser-options.png`): the siphon, a
coiled tendril, and a grasping cradle.

The builder is `docs/art/tentauri/scout/tools/tentauri_weapons.py`.
