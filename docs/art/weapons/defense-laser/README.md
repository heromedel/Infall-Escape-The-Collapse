# Tentauri Defense Laser

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Status:** accepted by Ship Designs, with the Tentauri scout. Weapon design: `docs/ship-suggestions.md`, "Tentauri
Defense Laser".

![Defense Laser at 4x](defense-laser-4x.png)

A siphon emitter: a smooth, tapered nacre tube on a soft seat, with a ringed mouth and a glowing teal lens. Drawn
top-down pointing up, centred on its mount point at (0, 0). Used twice on the Tentauri scout.

Three styles were tried, all drawn from the race's anatomy (`defense-laser-options.png`): the siphon (picked), a
coiled tendril, and a grasping cradle.

The builder is `docs/art/tentauri/scout/tools/tentauri_weapons.py`.
