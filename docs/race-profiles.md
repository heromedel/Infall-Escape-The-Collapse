# Infall: Race Profiles

Each race is defined with the profile below. Other race ideas are in `ideas.md`. Points are tagged as in
`tag-styles.md`.

How races treat each other: see Relations between races at the end.

## Profile template

1. **Racial tone and description:** who they are and how they feel to meet, including their ship style
   (hull shapes, colours) so their ships are recognisable at a glance.
2. **Base characteristics when doing fine.**
3. **Characteristics that emerge when struggling.**
4. **Characteristics when near the collapse and/or doomed.**
5. **How they interact with each other race.** This also drives how clusters change state (GDD 2L), e.g.
   whether they take in refugees or invade weakened neighbours.
6. **How their technology works or differs.**
7. **What it's like near and inside their territory.**
8. **Where and how they live:** whether they sprawl or hold a small, tight territory.
9. **Crew traits:** what one crew member of this race does on your ship, FTL-style (e.g. fights well,
   repairs fast). Ideally connected to the race's character.
10. **Ship designs across tiers:** basic to advanced, since a race can be placed anywhere from the core to the
    rim and its strength comes from its placement (GDD 2N).

## Terrans: The Galactic Alliance of Terran Occupied Systems ("the Gatos") (draft, 2026-10-21)

The starting race (GDD 7C). The name and its pun (*gatos*, "cats") are deliberate, to avoid a plain Federation
clone.

1. **Tone and ship style — Proposed (2026-10-21):** when doing well, clean, soft Federation-like ships. When
   desperate, a grim, survivalist look built from seemingly borrowed or stolen technology.
   - **Silhouette — Proposed (2026-10-22):** classic spaceship shapes, from large shuttles to cruisers and rockets.
   - **Colours — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** white and pale grey with blue trim; desperate ships add rust
     and mismatched panels from other races.
   - **Darker colours and scarier emblems — Decided (2026-10-23):** as the Gatos fall from struggling toward doomed,
     they use darker or black colours and scarier emblems.
   - **No standard emblem — Decided (2026-10-23):** there is no standard Gatos emblem, because they are an alliance,
     not a federation.
2. **Doing fine — Proposed (2026-10-21):** accepted by others, interested in trade and other cultures. Technology
   is clean, easy to use and humane.
3. **Struggling — Proposed (2026-10-21):** hopeful, somewhat optimistic, with the occasional pessimistic
   individual. They believe in coming together.
4. **Doomed — Proposed (2026-10-21):** aggressive and xenophobic; they blame their problems on others and resort
   to nearly any crime: theft, hijacking, using other races' technology, even piracy. Willing to do anything to
   survive.
5. **Relations — Proposed (2026-10-21):**
    - Doing well: likely to take in refugees of any race; helpful and diplomatic.
    - Struggling: less tolerant of aggressive races, less likely to take them in; more likely to resort to combat.
    - Doomed: refugees are among the things blamed; very unlikely to willingly take more in.
6. **Technology — Proposed (2026-10-21):** much like FTL's Federation, with **power conduits** and a heavily
   armoured power core (the **AZC**, Armoured ZeroPoint Core). The core has no power limit: if a system is
   connected, it has power, period; no power balancing and no maximum power. Instead, rooms can have these issues:
   atmosphere, fire, deck breaches, wall breaches and damaged conduits. Power must be rerouted through undamaged
   conduits or rooms, and cut conduits repaired. Ion weapons can still disrupt Terran systems, and systems can be
   switched off on purpose. Introduced slowly: the initial scout ship is easy to reroute, with routing mattering
   more on larger, higher-tier ships; it can be changed later if it becomes a problem.
   - **Balance idea — Proposed (2026-10-21):** starting from FTL's default weapon numbers, a weapon with a high
     hull-breach chance gets a lower chance of conduit damage; one with a low or no breach chance gets a
     conduit-damage chance, or a higher one.
   - **Synergy — Proposed (2026-10-21):** a power-giving crew race would be very strong on Terran ships, powering
     a room whose conduit was severed elsewhere in the ship.
   - **Setting — Proposed (2026-10-22):** `Terran Ship Conduits: On > Off` (default first). Off is FTL-like:
     Terran ships manage power like the biomechanical ships, with a limited pool of bars.
   - **Conversion — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** with the setting Off, each AZC level becomes 5 bars, the
     same equivalence `system-prices.md` already prices the AZC by; conduit damage then does nothing.
   - **Weapon names — Likely (2026-10-22):** an official designation plus a fun nickname, like real weapons
     (AK-47, "Ma Deuce"); e.g. Gat 3.1 "Triblast" (3 shots, 1 target), ".22" as a nickname for a light weapon.
     In Gatos codes M means missile (Gat M1), so enhanced models can use another marker, such as Gat 3.1 Mod2,
     V2, Model S, or EGat 3.1 (energy efficient); these are examples, not a fixed list.
7. **Near and inside their territory — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** core worlds are busy trade hubs:
   stores are common and well stocked, patrols keep pirates down, and doing-fine Terran clusters are the likeliest
   to become Refugee clusters (GDD 2R), since they take anyone in. Along the colony arms stores thin out and
   patrols are rare. When doomed, the same hubs become checkpoints with scans, tolls and shakedowns, and Terran
   pirates work the arms.
8. **Where and how they live — Proposed (2026-10-21):** both: tight-knit core areas plus arms/branches of
   colonies. Probably the most common race in the galaxy.
9. **Crew traits — Proposed (2026-10-21):** as FTL's humans: they gain skills faster.
10. **Ship tiers — Proposed (2026-10-21):** as FTL.

## Azurethea (draft, 2026-10-21)

1. **Tone and ship style — Proposed (2026-10-21):** a butterfly-like race of peaceful creatures using a mix of
   biomechanical technology. Ships shaped much like moths or butterflies, partly organic.
   - **Silhouette — Proposed (2026-10-22):** winged.
   - **Colours — Proposed (2026-10-22):** iridescent blues and royal blues with wing-like patterns.
2. **Doing fine — Proposed (2026-10-21):** nearly pacifist and docile; highly likely to accept or offer
   surrender; primarily interested in exploration. Slightly isolationist about sharing technology or letting
   others on their ships.
3. **Struggling — Proposed (2026-10-21):** more willing to cooperate and more interested in trade.
4. **Doomed — Proposed (2026-10-21):** optimistic but aggressively defensive; cooperate with peaceful races,
   become vengeful against violent enemies.
5. **Relations — Proposed (2026-10-21):** will willingly become refugees when needed, but less inclined to accept
   refugees, especially violent ones. Try to avoid war, but are decisive once in one.
6. **Technology — Proposed (2026-10-21):** part-organic ships. They produce their own Terran-friendly atmosphere
   in whatever room they stand in. Weapons often disable systems with spores, or fire damaging shots at single
   rooms. Power works as FTL's default (a limited pool of power that can be moved to any system), flavoured as
   biological energy flowing through the whole ship rather than a power core or conduits.
   - **Weapon names — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** biological, after creatures and natural things, like
     the Stinger.
7. **Near and inside their territory — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** quiet, lightly patrolled lanes along
   their veins, with exploration outposts at the tips. Their ships rarely start fights and readily accept
   surrender. Stores follow their mood: doing fine, they sell little of their own technology and few crew
   (isolationist); struggling, they open up and sell biomechanical systems and weapons (more interested in trade).
8. **Where and how they live — Proposed (2026-10-21):** sprawl in thin, almost veiny regions, reaching out for
   exploration or needed resources, and willingly leave unclaimed or neutral clusters near them.
9. **Crew traits — Proposed (2026-10-21):** half HP; produce breathable atmosphere; survive briefly outside a ship
   without an EVA suit, but cannot use EVA suits; repair biomechanical technology faster (most races struggle to
   repair it).
10. **Ship tiers — Proposed (2026-10-21):**
    - Low tech: single-system targeting shots (stinger missiles or single-shot energy beams).
    - Mid tech: spore systems or weapons that disable systems (cleared like a repair) and stun crew, with no breach
      or fire.
      Idea: their spores also mind control someone in the room, pacifying the enemy and making them fight each
      other. This would give mind control to the Azurethea. Possibly boarding crews, especially when they
      outnumber the other ship's crew. Higher evasion.
    - High tech: many weapon systems, missiles, a high-hull carapace, spore systems and weapons, good sensors,
      high evasion, medium shields at most.

## The Mecha (HalfMechs) (draft, 2026-10-21)

"The Mecha" is a working name that may become final.

- **Names — Proposed (2026-10-23):** they call themselves **HalfMechs**. Other races will still probably call them
  **The Mecha**.
- **What they are — Proposed (2026-10-23):** not robots. A small race of childlike, genetically modified cyborgs who
  live inside semi-cybernetic mech suits. Outside of their suits they are rather attractive for underdeveloped
  half-cybernetic creatures, due to their genetic enhancement and quick growth cycle, like diminutive genetically
  engineered humans, and oddly adorable for their viciousness.
- **Origin — Proposed (2026-10-23):** lost to time and somewhat of a mystery, but genetic evidence suggests they
  were Terran, bred to better fit inside semi-cybernetic mech suits. An accidental by-product of their creation,
  and possibly their personal power inside the mechs, made them immature, violent and greedy, like cruel children.
- **Out of their suits — Proposed (2026-10-23):** incredibly bashful if not violent. Not truly shy, though: their
  overconfidence tends to override it.
- **What others know — Proposed (2026-10-23):** most races are likely not fully aware of any of this. Some rumors
  claim they are hideous and evil, others that they are adorable and just greedy or immature.
- **Organized and tactical — Proposed (2026-10-23):** because they want power and wealth, and they will work together
  to get it.

1. **Tone — Proposed (2026-10-21):** an aggressive destruction race that guts ships; highly organized and
   tactical.
   - **Silhouette — Proposed (2026-10-22):** wide, aggressive battleships.
   - **Colours — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** gunmetal and black with yellow and orange hazard markings.
2. **Doing fine — Proposed (2026-10-21):** territorial and aggressive. If outmatched, they may insist the
   interloper leave their territory. If they have the advantage they are likely to attack, only accepting
   surrender if they profit from it and are injured themselves.
3. **Struggling — Proposed (2026-10-21):** more likely to agree to trades or surrenders they can profit from or
   gain a power advantage from.
4. **Doomed — Proposed (2026-10-21):** still expect at least the appearance of an advantage or benefit for
   themselves; still not generous or charitable, but more inclined to teamwork when their survival depends on it.
5. **Relations — Proposed (2026-10-21):** not likely to become refugees unless absolutely desperate, and even then
   more likely to become raiders or pirates.
6. **Technology — Proposed (2026-10-21):** weapons tend to be high damage and destructive to hulls, lacking the
   finesse other races might prefer. Instead of an EVA Dock they have an **EVB Dock** (Extra Vehicular Battle
   Dock): a variant of the EVA Dock, basically battle EVA suits. It needs a battlesuit type, the way a drone bay
   needs a drone type, and likely costs material to launch. Crew fly out to ships themselves to attack (or do
   other things) repeatedly. Other races can use it if they acquire one.
   - **Suit oxygen — Likely (2026-10-23):** a battlesuit has a limited supply of its own oxygen. It starts going
     down as soon as the suit leaves the ship and keeps going down on the way back; if it runs out, the occupant
     slowly dies of asphyxiation.
   - **A hit suit — Proposed (2026-10-23):** a suit that is shot probably survives the first hit, damaged and
     unusable. A damaged suit cannot fire. After a brief stun it can return to a ship in its squad. A suit can be
     taken off on any ship; if that ship has no way to send its occupant back, that is the player's problem.
   - **A second hit — Likely (2026-10-23):** destroys the suit and its occupant.
   - **Boarding suits — Proposed (2026-10-23):** a boarding suit can be worn aboard another ship, fill up with
     oxygen there, and come back from any airlock.
   - **No repairs — Likely (2026-10-23):** suits are not normally repaired. A suit vanishes when it is taken off
     (lore: it is recycled into a little energy), and a new one is paid for at an EVB Dock.
   - **A new suit every launch — Likely (2026-10-23):** every launch takes a new suit, the way FTL uses a drone part
     every time a drone is deployed.
   - **Recycling refund — Proposed (2026-10-23):** most likely none. If there is one, it is about 0.01 Mawjig, and
     the suit's cost goes up by the same amount.
   - **Skills and power vs suits — Decided (2026-10-23):** everything about flying around in battle is the EVB
     suit; skills and extra power belong to the Mecha themselves.
   - **Tech type — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** mechanical, which is why integrating with biomechanical
     systems takes them longer (point 9).
   - **Weapon names — Proposed (2026-10-22):** descriptive and functional, e.g. Triple Shot - Single Target -
     Light Laser.
   - **Weapon card code — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** a short code on the card, e.g. TS-ST Light Laser,
     with the full name as the official designation.
7. **Near and inside their territory — Proposed (2026-10-21):** in their core territory they help their own kind
   build, upgrade and repair. In their inner territories they may battle each other if one side appears weak or
   suspicious. Their border territories are typically in constant battle with whoever they border, and with
   criminals and pirates from other regions. Since they don't actively seek out criminals, only attacking when
   they run into them or see an advantage, some races find it worthwhile to hide inside Mecha border clusters.
8. **Where and how they live — Proposed (2026-10-21):** they expand like lakes or rivers, flowing into the regions
   of least resistance, preferring previously populated areas over empty regions where they would have to build
   infrastructure themselves. This gives criminals another place to hide, but to the Mecha, criminals developing
   empty clusters just makes them better to take later.
9. **Crew traits — Proposed (2026-10-21):** integrate with any ship system, powering it by one bar, similar to
   FTL's Zoltan; Mecha could perhaps be upgraded to power two bars. Integrating takes a moment longer to begin
   using a system and to disengage, and even longer with biomechanical systems. Slow movement, high melee damage,
   physically tough. Mecha ships have no reactor; if subsystems need power, the system has its own independent
   power. A single weapon system is hard for them to power, so their ships need multiple weapon systems or, more
   likely, rely on EVB suits.
   - **Breathing — Likely (2026-10-23):** Mecha breathe. Suffocation is their key weakness: taking out their life
     support is a legitimate tactic, and one of them has to disengage and move to repair it.
   - **No overpowering — Decided (2026-10-23):** integration never powers a system above its level.
   - **Mech Repair Bay (working name; needs a better one) — Likely (2026-10-23):** Mecha ships have no strong
     power core, but they have a Mech Repair Bay. It is both the Mecha's Medbay and where they upgrade their suits
     to provide more power, so a Mecha crew member can be upgraded to power a second bar.
   - **Skills capped by their mech — Likely (2026-10-23):** Mecha learn skills, possibly faster than other races,
     but can only use a skill up to the level their own mech is upgraded to. They pay Mawjig at the Mech Repair Bay
     to upgrade their mech to match their abilities. Replaces the earlier proposal that they pay Mawjig for the
     skills themselves and otherwise learn very slowly like the Senti-GPH.
10. **Ship tiers — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):**
    - Low tech: one EVB Dock with laser battlesuits, plus one or two self-powered weapon systems with a
      hull-damage laser (Hull Smasher-like). Thin shields.
    - Mid tech: boarding battlesuits, breach missiles or bombs, a second EVB Dock or weapon system, more hull.
    - High tech: several or upgraded EVB Docks with beam battlesuits, heavy beams (Halberd or Glaive-like) and the
      most hull of any race; shields stay average. Their strength is gutting ships, not out-shielding them.

## Tentauri (draft, 2026-10-21)

Octopus-like race (earlier working label "Octopods").

1. **Tone and ship style — Proposed (2026-10-21):** octopus-like beings in small groups. Small crews and ships,
   perhaps two crew members on a standard ship, possibly mates. Their ships have few rooms with many systems in
   them.
   - **Silhouette — Proposed (2026-10-22):** compact, like tiny shuttles.
   - **Colours — Proposed (2026-10-22):** prismatic, multicoloured ships, close to a chameleon or mother of
     pearl; aqua, teal and coral white or coral pink among them, in mottled patterns.
2. **Doing fine — Proposed (2026-10-21):** quiet and diplomatic, but if they want to go into a system they are
   willing to force their way in; not looking to destroy others, simply to get where they want to go, for
   whatever reason.
3. **Struggling — Proposed (2026-10-21):** willing to ally, usually in joint operations more than trade, e.g.
   having a ship join a fleet rather than gifting a ship.
4. **Doomed — Proposed (2026-10-21):** highly cooperative, possibly even allying with aggressive races.
5. **Relations — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** they rarely take refugees aboard, having little room, but
   readily escort refugee convoys through dangerous clusters (the joint operations of point 3). Doing fine, they
   trespass but avoid wars; struggling or doomed, they join other races' fleets, even aggressive ones.
6. **Technology — Proposed (2026-10-21):** most likely biomechanical, with the same power system as the
   Azurethea; they specialise in shields and defence. If there is a Zoltan-like shield (an extra energy shield
   layer), it would probably go to the Tentauri. Weapons tend to be standard lasers, beams or missiles as needed;
   they don't mind adapting.
   - **Weapon names — Proposed (2026-10-22):** from their nature as an evolved cephalopod-like invertebrate,
     tentacled race, not ocean themes.
   - **Example names — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** Coil, Grasp, Siphon, Tendril Beam.
7. **Near and inside their territory — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** few, far-apart regions of a handful
   of clusters each, heavily shielded and calm. Their stores stock shields, defence and Backup Batteries. Outside
   their regions, lone Tentauri ships are a common sight passing through other races' territory.
8. **Where and how they live — Proposed (2026-10-21):** explorers who spread by starting small regions further
   apart from each other than most races.
9. **Crew traits — Proposed (2026-10-21):** can run multiple systems at the same time, maybe up to four.
10. **Ship tiers:**
    - **Low tech — Proposed (2026-10-22):** the Tough Little Scout (`ship-suggestions.md`): 2 crew, two rooms,
      one shield layer, Supershield, two Defense Lasers.
    - **Mid tech — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** 2–3 crew, three rooms; two shield layers and a faster
      Supershield; defence that also shoots down lasers (like FTL's Defense Drone Mark II); a Pike-like beam;
      Backup Battery.
    - **High tech — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** 4 crew, still few rooms; three shield layers plus
      Supershield; Halberd-like beam and Artemis-like missiles.
    - **Protecting a squad-mate — Proposed (2026-10-22):** at high tech, either a shield beam projected onto
      another ship or a Supershield missile that gives a squad-mate a Supershield layer (GDD 3N).

## Senti-GPH (Sentient Generative Pretrained Holograms) (draft, 2026-10-21)

**Names — Decided (2026-10-23):**

- **Technical name:** Sentient Generative Pretrained Holograms.
- **Full name:** Senti-GPH. Not usually used by people actually speaking.
- **Short name:** Senti, The Senti, or The Senti-.
- A single Senti speaks in the first person and uses words like I and me, but in negotiations they have a habit of
  saying "We, The Senti-". For example, a store's opening line: "We, The Senti-, welcome you to look around our
  storage facilities if you would like to trade."
- The dash in "The Senti-" is intentional and stays: it stands for the letters after the dash, partly so players
  don't have GPH shoved in their faces constantly.

1. **Tone and description — Proposed (2026-10-21):** holographic AI avatars, originally made by Terrans, who
   became their own people. They build, grow and multiply like other races; they archive information because
   they are made of information themselves.
   - **Silhouette — Proposed (2026-10-22):** Terran-like, but centralized around the AAA.
   - **Colours — Proposed (2026-10-22):** cyan or cyan-green holographic light lines.
   - **Base colour — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** Terran white under the light lines.
2. **Doing fine — Proposed (2026-10-21):** curious and logical; collect and preserve knowledge from every culture
   they meet.
3. **Struggling — Proposed (2026-10-21):** preserving data comes first; they trade for records, archives and
   storage, and also for things necessary for survival, such as defensive equipment as needed.
4. **Doomed — Proposed (2026-10-21):** no qualms about killing living people, while still willing to store their
   information and biological data.
5. **Relations — Proposed (2026-10-21):** taking in refugees costs them life support they don't otherwise need,
   so they would rather preserve a refugee race's records than house its people.
6. **Technology — Proposed (2026-10-21):** very similar to the Terrans', including the same power conduits, with
   advanced beam weapons and sensors. Besides an armoured core (the **AZC**, Armoured ZeroPoint Core, as on
   Terran ships, though maybe slightly worse), they have an **Armoured Avatar Array** (the **AAA** system) that
   stores and broadcasts their holographic avatars. If it is damaged, fewer avatars can be broadcast, so the ship
   has fewer holographic crew until it is repaired; they don't die from this unless the ship is destroyed. They
   don't usually use ion weapons themselves.
   - **Weapon names — Proposed (2026-10-22):** number codes. Beams give rooms swept, type, damage and version,
     e.g. 3B 01 V2 (3-room beam, 1 damage, version 2). Other weapons can start with the shot count instead (e.g.
     2L 02 V1, a 2-shot laser with 2 damage); a guideline, not an absolute rule.
7. **Near and inside their territory — Proposed (2026-10-21):** small, dense clusters built around relay and
   broadcast stations.
8. **Where and how they live — Proposed (2026-10-21):** see 7; they stay close together, since their avatars
   depend on arrays.
9. **Crew traits — Proposed (2026-10-21):** half HP individually; don't need oxygen; immune to fire. Bound to
   their ship's AAA, but a highly upgraded AAA can broadcast them directly onto another ship, with a cooldown.
   Ion disrupts them.
   Idea: their skill training is chosen in advance, when recruited or acquired; they cost more to recruit or
   acquire, especially since a ship needs an AAA of a minimum level to host even one; and they learn very poorly
   on their own (massively reduced skill gain).
10. **Ship tiers — Proposed (2026-10-21):**
    - Low: beams, good sensors, a small AAA.
    - Mid: hacking, a larger AAA.
    - High: more hacking, an upgraded AAA, shield-penetrating technologies or weapons; not likely missiles.

## Lykario (draft, 2026-10-21)

Wolf-like race (earlier working label "wolf race").

1. **Description and ship style — Proposed (2026-10-21):** evolved independently, but similar to Terran canines.
   Ships are a mix of stolen parts, but efficient when doing well.
   - **Silhouette — Proposed (2026-10-22):** Terran-like, but more rugged and hostile.
   - **Colours — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** a patchwork of stolen hull colours under their own
     bone-white claw markings.
2. **Doing fine — Proposed (2026-10-21):** raid and attack others, seemingly for fun; willing to join up with
   others for a joint hunt.
3. **Struggling — Proposed (2026-10-21):** tend to be in denial, insisting things are fine and just wanting to
   continue the hunt, but even more willing to trade or join up.
4. **Doomed — Proposed (2026-10-21):** oddly vicious but optimistic and highly willing to team up, convinced they
   will be fine as long as they just keep going.
5. **Relations — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** they don't take in refugees as such, but take in anyone who
   proves useful on a hunt: refugees who fight beside them can join the pack, the rest are prey. They rarely
   become refugees themselves; doomed bands follow refugee convoys instead.
6. **Technology — Proposed (2026-10-21):** a mix of non-biomechanical tech stolen from others; basic weapons and
   technology; crew teleporters; cloaking. Their power model is whatever the ship they stole uses. They tend to
   use an autopilot while boarding others.
   - **Weapon names — Proposed (2026-10-22):** most of their weapons are stolen and unmodified, so they keep their
     maker's name. Only the few they edit or build themselves get Lykario names.
   - **Lykario names — Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-10-22):** hunting words, e.g. "Fang".
7. **Near and inside their territory — Proposed (2026-10-21):** see 8.
8. **Where and how they live — Proposed (2026-10-21):** small, localized, independent clusters that even attack
   each other.
9. **Crew traits — Proposed (2026-10-21):** quick attacks, reduced HP, Animal Senses.
   - **Animal Senses — Decided (2026-10-23):** as if they are psychic, but player-facing text never calls it
     psychic. It is always "Animal Senses", and the senses are never explained. They sense Life Signs, without
     using ship sensors and even on other ships and in hazards.
10. **Ship tiers — Proposed (2026-10-21):** even their best ship designs are not that amazing, aside from larger or
    multiple crew teleporter rooms.

## Malphen (formally Malphagius) (idea, 2026-10-21)

Tumor race. "Malphagius" (roughly "the malignant devourer") is the formal or scientific name, used in lore and
events; "Malphen" is the everyday name.

- **Proposed (2026-10-21):** basically a core race.
- **Silhouette — Proposed (2026-10-22):** bulbous, irregular shapes.
- **Proposed (2026-10-21):** biomechanical weapons that infect a ship's systems and spread.
- **Open (2026-10-21):** either the race is basically mutants, or it is a living ship with no crew. (An answer is
  suggested in the profile below.)
- **Proposed (2026-10-21):** weapons tend to be fired like fleshy missiles or like flak guns, to get through
  shields.
- **Proposed (2026-10-21):** maybe they have the equivalent of the clone bay.
- **Proposed (2026-10-21):** should probably have high HP and clone themselves.
- **Proposed (2026-10-21):** idea: a mutation system that lets Malphen crew swap their specialty, e.g.
  anti-personnel, repair, fire.
- **Proposed (2026-10-21):** idea: launching is a mutation. A Malphen mutates to survive the launch, then goes to
  an airlock or a launching bay and is fired into another ship. If it is just an airlock, that explains how they
