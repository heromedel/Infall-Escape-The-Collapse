# Infall: Lore Facts

What the world of Infall knows: one fact per line, each with its source. Facts are copied (and clarified) from the
other docs, not moved; the source doc stays the home of the design. Game mechanics, numbers and design notes do not
belong here.

Every new event, outcome and message is checked against this file. It is only edited with the designer's
permission; new facts are offered as a numbered list first.

Tags: **heromedel** = the designer's own fact. **AI draft** = written by Claude, not yet approved. An approved AI
draft becomes **approved by heromedel**.

## The galaxy and the collapse

- The galaxy's core has collapsed into a super black hole. (GDD 1B; heromedel)
- The collapse horizon expands outward from the core, swallowing star clusters as it goes. (GDD 2A, 2P; heromedel)
- Before the collapse, some races sensed it coming, and astronomers detected something wrong in the core. (GDD 2P; heromedel)
- Unusual radiation emanated from the core before it collapsed. (GDD 2P; heromedel)
- The core then grew brighter than it had ever been, and communication with regions closer to the core was interrupted. (GDD 2P; heromedel)
- When the core went dark, jumps heading away from the core increased tenfold. (GDD 2P; heromedel)
- Near the collapse, people are in shock and scrambling to leave their planets. (GDD 2B; heromedel)
- Worlds far from the collapse may not believe it is real. (GDD 2B; heromedel)
- Stars are grouped into star clusters, and related clusters form regions, such as a civilization's territory. (GDD 2J; heromedel)
- A region has deep, inner and border clusters, and unclaimed or neutral clusters can lie between regions. (GDD 2K; heromedel)
- Only the most powerful factions, and those who band together, are making it far toward the rim. (GDD 3G; heromedel)

## The player's people

- A giant colony ship carries the last survivors of the player's home planet toward the rim. (GDD 1D; heromedel)
- Scout ships fly ahead of the colony ship, scouting and mapping the way, and stay in communication with it. (GDD 1D; heromedel)

## Mawjig and jump travel

- Mawjig is matter converted into stored energy. It fuels jump drives and weapons and is used as currency. (GDD 8C; heromedel)
- The word Mawjig began as a Terran acronym: Materials Worth of Jigawatts. (GDD 8C; heromedel)
- Wreckage and other matter can be converted into Mawjig. (GDD 8C; heromedel)
- The Mawjig Drive is the common jump drive. Some races have their own variants, but all of them burn Mawjig. (GDD 6A; heromedel)

## Technology

- Technology comes in types, such as mechanical and biomechanical. Biomechanical technology is part organic. (GDD 8D; heromedel)
- Most races struggle to repair biomechanical technology, but the Azurethea repair it quickly. (GDD 8D; heromedel)

## Terrans (the Gatos)

- The Terrans' formal name is the Galactic Alliance of Terran Occupied Systems, known as the Gatos. (Race profiles, Terrans; heromedel)
- Terrans are believed to be the most common race in the galaxy. (Race profiles, Terrans 8; heromedel)
- Terrans live in tight-knit core areas, with arms of colonies reaching out from them. (Race profiles, Terrans 8; heromedel)
- In good times Terrans are accepted by others and are interested in trade and other cultures. (Race profiles, Terrans 2; heromedel)
- Terran technology is known for being clean, easy to use and humane. (Race profiles, Terrans 2; heromedel)
- Struggling Terrans stay hopeful and believe in coming together. (Race profiles, Terrans 3; heromedel)
- Doomed Terrans turn aggressive and xenophobic, blame others, and resort to theft, hijacking, stolen technology and piracy. (Race profiles, Terrans 4; heromedel)
- Terrans who are doing well take in refugees of any race. Doomed Terrans count refugees among the things they blame. (Race profiles, Terrans 5; heromedel)
- Terran ships are powered by an Armoured ZeroPoint Core (AZC) through power conduits. (Race profiles, Terrans 6; heromedel)
- Terran ships are clean and soft in good times. Desperate Terran ships look grim, built from borrowed or stolen technology. (Race profiles, Terrans 1; heromedel)
- Terran ships are white and pale grey with blue trim. Desperate ones add rust and mismatched panels from other races. (Race profiles, Terrans 1; AI draft)
- Terran weapons carry an official designation and a nickname, such as Gat 3.1 "Triblast". In Gatos codes, M means missile. (Race profiles, Terrans 6; heromedel)
- Terran core worlds are busy trade hubs with patrols that keep pirates down, while the colony arms are thinly patrolled. (Race profiles, Terrans 7; AI draft)
- When the Terrans are doomed, their trade hubs become checkpoints with scans, tolls and shakedowns. (Race profiles, Terrans 7; AI draft)
- Terrans are quick learners. (Race profiles, Terrans 9; heromedel)

## Azurethea

- The Azurethea are a peaceful, butterfly-like race. (Race profiles, Azurethea 1; heromedel)
- Azurethea ships are partly organic and shaped like moths or butterflies. (Race profiles, Azurethea 1; heromedel)
- Azurethea ships are iridescent blue and royal blue, with wing-like patterns. (Race profiles, Azurethea 1; AI draft)
- In good times the Azurethea are nearly pacifist explorers who readily accept or offer surrender. (Race profiles, Azurethea 2; heromedel)
- The Azurethea are slow to share their technology or let others aboard their ships. (Race profiles, Azurethea 2; heromedel)
- Struggling Azurethea become more willing to cooperate and trade. (Race profiles, Azurethea 3; heromedel)
- Doomed Azurethea stay optimistic but become aggressively defensive, and vengeful against violent enemies. (Race profiles, Azurethea 4; heromedel)
- The Azurethea willingly become refugees when they must, but are reluctant to take in refugees, especially violent ones. (Race profiles, Azurethea 5; heromedel)
- The Azurethea try to avoid war, but are decisive once in one. (Race profiles, Azurethea 5; heromedel)
- An Azurethea produces breathable air wherever it stands and can survive briefly in space without a suit. (Race profiles, Azurethea 6, 9; heromedel)
- Azurethea ships run on biological energy flowing through the whole ship, not on a power core. (Race profiles, Azurethea 6; heromedel)
- Azurethea weapons often disable systems with spores. (Race profiles, Azurethea 6; heromedel)
- Azurethea weapons are named after creatures and natural things, such as the Stinger. (Race profiles, Azurethea 6; AI draft)
- The Azurethea spread in thin, vein-like regions reaching out for exploration and resources, and leave the neutral clusters near them alone. (Race profiles, Azurethea 8; heromedel)

## The Mecha

- The Mecha are an aggressive, highly organized and tactical race known for gutting ships. (Race profiles, Mecha 1; heromedel)
- Mecha ships are gunmetal and black with yellow and orange hazard markings. (Race profiles, Mecha 1; AI draft)
- The Mecha are territorial. When outmatched they insist interlopers leave, and when they have the advantage they attack. (Race profiles, Mecha 2; heromedel)
- The Mecha accept a surrender only when they profit from it. (Race profiles, Mecha 2, 3; heromedel)
- Doomed Mecha still expect an advantage for themselves, but turn to teamwork when their survival depends on it. (Race profiles, Mecha 4; heromedel)
- The Mecha rarely become refugees. Desperate Mecha become raiders or pirates instead. (Race profiles, Mecha 5; heromedel)
- Mecha fight in battle EVA suits launched from an Extra Vehicular Battle Dock (EVB Dock). (Race profiles, Mecha 6; heromedel)
- Mecha weapon names are descriptive and functional, such as Triple Shot - Single Target - Light Laser. (Race profiles, Mecha 6; heromedel)
- In their core territory the Mecha help their own kind build, upgrade and repair. (Race profiles, Mecha 7; heromedel)
- In their inner territories the Mecha may fight each other if one side looks weak or suspicious. (Race profiles, Mecha 7; heromedel)
- Mecha border clusters are in constant battle with their neighbours and with criminals and pirates. (Race profiles, Mecha 7; heromedel)
- Criminals hide in Mecha border clusters, since the Mecha don't seek them out. (Race profiles, Mecha 7; heromedel)
- The Mecha flow like water into the regions of least resistance, preferring previously populated areas to empty ones. (Race profiles, Mecha 8; heromedel)
- A Mecha can integrate with a ship system and power it. (Race profiles, Mecha 9; heromedel)
- The Mecha are slow-moving, physically tough and dangerous in melee. (Race profiles, Mecha 9; heromedel)

## Tentauri

- The Tentauri are an octopus-like race living in small groups, often two to a ship, possibly mates. (Race profiles, Tentauri 1; heromedel)
- Tentauri ships are small and compact, with few rooms holding many systems. (Race profiles, Tentauri 1; heromedel)
- Tentauri ships are prismatic and mottled, like a chameleon or mother of pearl. (Race profiles, Tentauri 1; heromedel)
- The Tentauri are quiet and diplomatic, but force their way into any place they want to go, without looking to destroy anyone. (Race profiles, Tentauri 2; heromedel)
- Struggling Tentauri ally in joint operations, such as a ship joining a fleet, more than they trade. (Race profiles, Tentauri 3; heromedel)
- Doomed Tentauri are highly cooperative, even with aggressive races. (Race profiles, Tentauri 4; heromedel)
- The Tentauri rarely take refugees aboard, but readily escort refugee convoys through dangerous clusters. (Race profiles, Tentauri 5; AI draft)
- Tentauri technology is mostly biomechanical, and they specialise in shields and defence. (Race profiles, Tentauri 6; heromedel)
- Tentauri weapon names come from their own anatomy, such as Coil, Grasp, Siphon and Tendril Beam. (Race profiles, Tentauri 6; AI draft)
- The Tentauri are explorers who found small regions far apart from each other. (Race profiles, Tentauri 8; heromedel)
- Lone Tentauri ships are a common sight passing through other races' territory. (Race profiles, Tentauri 7; AI draft)
- A Tentauri can work several systems at once. (Race profiles, Tentauri 9; heromedel)

## Senti-GPH

- The Senti-GPH (Sentient Generative Pre-trained Holograms) are holographic AI avatars originally made by Terrans, who became their own people. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 1; heromedel)
- The Senti-GPH build, grow and multiply like other races. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 1; heromedel)
- The Senti-GPH archive information because they are made of information themselves. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 1; heromedel)
- Senti-GPH ships are Terran white with cyan or cyan-green holographic light lines. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 1; AI draft)
- In good times the Senti-GPH are curious and logical, collecting and preserving knowledge from every culture they meet. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 2; heromedel)
- Struggling Senti-GPH put preserving data first, trading for records, archives and storage. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 3; heromedel)
- Doomed Senti-GPH have no qualms about killing living people, while still storing their information and biological data. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 4; heromedel)
- The Senti-GPH would rather preserve a refugee race's records than house its people, since housing them costs life support they don't otherwise need. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 5; heromedel)
- Senti-GPH ships broadcast their people from an Armoured Avatar Array (AAA), alongside an AZC like the Terrans'. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 6; heromedel)
- Senti-GPH weapon names are number codes, such as 3B 01 V2. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 6; heromedel)
- The Senti-GPH live in small, dense clusters built around relay and broadcast stations. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 7, 8; heromedel)
- The Senti-GPH don't breathe, can't burn and are disrupted by ion. (Race profiles, Senti-GPH 9; heromedel)

## Lykario

- The Lykario are a wolf-like race that evolved independently but resembles Terran canines. (Race profiles, Lykario 1; heromedel)
- Lykario ships are built from stolen parts. (Race profiles, Lykario 1; heromedel)
- Lykario ships are a patchwork of stolen hull colours under bone-white claw markings. (Race profiles, Lykario 1; AI draft)
- In good times the Lykario raid others seemingly for fun, and will join others for a joint hunt. (Race profiles, Lykario 2; heromedel)
- Struggling Lykario deny that anything is wrong and just want to continue the hunt. (Race profiles, Lykario 3; heromedel)
- Doomed Lykario are oddly vicious but optimistic, convinced they will be fine as long as they keep going. (Race profiles, Lykario 4; heromedel)
- The Lykario take in anyone who proves useful on a hunt. Those who don't are prey. (Race profiles, Lykario 5; AI draft)
- Most Lykario weapons are stolen and keep their maker's name. (Race profiles, Lykario 6; heromedel)
- The Lykario live in small, independent clusters that even attack each other. (Race profiles, Lykario 8; heromedel)
- The Lykario have Animal Sense: their smell and hearing are keen enough to sense people through walls. (Race profiles, Lykario 9; heromedel)

## Malphen (Malphagius)

- The Malphen are a tumor race. Their formal name, Malphagius, roughly means "the malignant devourer". (Race profiles, Malphen; heromedel)
- Malphen weapons infect a ship's systems, and the infection spreads. (Race profiles, Malphen; heromedel)
- When the Malphen can't win or escape, they launch valuable debris for the other side to take and try to flee, which is their way of surrendering. (Race profiles, Malphen; heromedel)
- Each Malphen ship is a living organism, and its crew are parts it grows. (Race profiles, Malphen 1; AI draft)
- The Malphen don't speak. (Race profiles, Malphen 1; AI draft)
- The Malphen grow into empty and abandoned clusters and absorb wrecks. (Race profiles, Malphen 2; AI draft)
- The Malphen can be paid in matter to let a ship pass. (Race profiles, Malphen 2; AI draft)
- No race takes the Malphen in. The Azurethea and Tentauri fear them most. (Race profiles, Malphen 5; AI draft)
- Malphen weapons are named by the races who face them, such as the Flesh Mortar. (Race profiles, Malphen 6; AI draft)
- Malphen territory is one connected mass that slowly grows outward. (Race profiles, Malphen 8; AI draft)

## Hidden from the world

True in the world, but no one in it knows. Events must not reveal these unless the player discovers them.

- The Whispwraith are beings that seem to be made of a cloud of particles and energy. (Race profiles, Whispwraith; heromedel)
- No other race knows the Whispwraith exist. They likely live undiscovered inside the collapse. (Race profiles, Whispwraith 5; heromedel)
- Oxygen atmospheres are toxic to the Whispwraith, and their own atmosphere is harmful to other races. (Race profiles, Whispwraith; heromedel)
- Whispwraith weapon names are their own, highly strange and alien. (Race profiles, Whispwraith 6; heromedel)
- The deeper into the collapse, the better the Whispwraith do. (Race profiles, Whispwraith 2; AI draft)
