## The task

Write, in the format of `events-sample.md` and through `event-design-process.md`:

1. **The five collapse opening messages** (GDD 2P, Likely): one per jump for the first five jumps of a run, each
   laid over whatever ordinary event the cluster has. They are messages, not events with choices: an OPENING box
   and a "Continue" is enough, unless step 3 of the process gives one of them a real choice. They must follow 2P's
   five beats exactly: the start (your race sensed the collapse, or its astronomers found something wrong in the
   core, and sent the colony ship with you as scouts); unusual radiation from the core; the core brighter than ever
   and communication with inner regions interrupted; the core dark and jumps away from the core up tenfold; whole
   regions swallowed, the collapse now visible on the map and clusters that looked reachable swallowed at once.
   Goal A's player is Terran (the Gatos), so write them for a Terran squad; where a line would differ for another
   race, mark it `{race}` or note it.
2. **Three Terran store texts** for a doing-fine Gatos cluster (race profile, Terrans 2 and 7: trade hubs, patrols,
   well-stocked stores; clean, easy, humane technology), as the opening box of a store event, three variants of one
   event, the way `events-sample.md` writes variant openings. Doomed and struggling variants are not part of this
   batch.
