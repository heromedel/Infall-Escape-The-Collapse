## The collapse opening (GDD 2P)

Five messages, one for each of the first five clusters of a run, in 2P's order. Each is laid over the cluster's
ordinary event: the message box comes first, and "Continue..." leads into the cluster's own event.

| Step | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1. Band, cluster, type | Band 1, the first five clusters of every run. A message, not an event of its own. |
| 2. Cluster states | Any. It sits on top of whatever the cluster holds. |
| 3. Options | None: each is one box and "Continue...". None of the five beats needs a decision from the player, and a choice here would only delay the cluster's own event. |
| 4. Involves | The colony ship, which the scouts stay in communication with (GDD 1D). |
| 5. Meant to | Tell the start of the collapse a step at a time, so the player feels it build from a strange reading to the end of the core. Set up the colony ship as the voice behind the scouts. |
| 6. Band scaling | None. |
| 7. Specific clusters | None. Written for a Terran (Gatos) squad; lines that would change for another race are noted. |
| 8. Voice | The narrator, plain and informative, for what the scouts see. The colony ship's bridge for the transmissions: steady and hopeful (struggling Terrans believe in coming together), more shaken with each message. |

```
COLLAPSE_OPENING_1: "Behind You" (the first cluster)
Your homeworld is in a panic. Something is wrong with the Galactic Core, the region at the center of the galaxy.
Scientists are in a frenzy of arguments and debates, but they all agree that something cataclysmic could be coming.
Many refuse to believe it, but others have sponsored a colony ship to travel farther from the core. Your job, as
Captain of the forward scouts, is to explore ahead and find a path for The Colony Ship to survive.

Behind you, The Colony Ship's engines light up as it begins its long journey. A message comes through from its
bridge.

"Our astronomers still can't say what is wrong with the Galactic Core. They can only say it is getting worse. We
won't wait to find out. Everyone we could bring is aboard. Scout the way ahead, find us safe clusters to jump to,
and keep in touch. We'll be right behind you."

1. Continue...

THEN: the cluster's own event.
```

```
COLLAPSE_OPENING_2: "Radiation" (the second cluster)
A transmission from The Colony Ship is waiting for you when you arrive.

"Our sensors are picking up unusual radiation from the core. Nobody aboard has seen readings like these before.
Keep moving. We'll send word as soon as we understand it."

1. Continue...

THEN: the cluster's own event.
```

```
COLLAPSE_OPENING_3: "Brighter" (the third cluster)
The Galactic Core is brighter than anyone has ever seen it. Even from here, its glare washes out the stars around
it on your viewscreens.

The Colony Ship's signal comes through weaker than before. "Our messages to the inner regions aren't getting
through. Their relays have gone quiet, all of them at once. Whatever is happening, it is reaching them first."

1. Continue...

THEN: the cluster's own event.
```

```
COLLAPSE_OPENING_4: "Dark" (the fourth cluster)
The core has gone dark. Where the glare was, there is only black.

"Our long-range sensors are tracking jumps heading away from the core," The Colony Ship reports. "Ten times as
many as before it went dark. Everyone out there saw what we saw, and they are all running."

1. Continue...

THEN: the cluster's own event.
```

```
COLLAPSE_OPENING_5: "Swallowed" (the fifth cluster)
Your sensors show it before The Colony Ship's message arrives. The Galactic Core has collapsed into a super black
hole, and its edge is moving outward. Clusters that looked like an easy few jumps inward are gone, swallowed all at
once.

"It's real. It's coming our way," The Colony Ship says. "We'll follow your path as fast as our Jigawatt Drives
allow. Find us a way out."

1. Continue...

THEN: the collapse appears on the map. The cluster's own event follows.
```

Opening 1's first paragraph is heromedel's text (2026-10-23), with spelling and grammar fixed.

**Other races:**

- Opening 1: "Our astronomers" fits the Terrans. 2P lets a race sense the collapse instead, so a race that senses
  it would say something like "We can feel that something is wrong with the galactic core" (`{race}` line).
- Opening 1: "Everyone we could bring is aboard" assumes a crewed colony ship, which suits every race so far.
- The colony ship's voice is written as Terran. Another race's colony ship would speak in that race's voice, e.g.
  the Senti-GPH's negotiation habit doesn't apply here, since these are reports, not negotiations.

**Checks:**

- Lore used: the five beats of the collapse opening (GDD 2P, Likely). The core collapses into a super black hole
  (GDD 1B, Decided). The scouts fly ahead of the colony ship and stay in communication with it (GDD 1D, Decided).
  The colony ship carries the last survivors of the home planet (GDD 1D, Decided). The collapse becomes visible on
  the map in the fifth message (GDD 2P). Struggling Terrans stay hopeful and believe in coming together (race
  profile, Terrans 3; Proposed). The drive is the Jigawatt Drive (GDD 6D, Decided).
- "Ten times as many" in opening 4 is 2P's tenfold. It is part of the story, not an amount the player gains or
  spends.

## STORE_TERRAN_DOING_FINE: "A Gatos Trade Hub"

| Step | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1. Band, cluster, type | Any band. A doing-fine Gatos core or inner cluster with a store. A store's opening box. |
| 2. Cluster states | Doing Fine. Struggling and doomed versions are a later batch. |
| 3. Options | "Continue..." opens the store. |
| 4. Involves | A busy Gatos trade hub and the people who run it. |
| 5. Meant to | Show the Gatos at their best: open, organized and glad to trade. Give the player a safe, welcoming stop that later Struggling and Doomed versions can be measured against. |
| 6. Band scaling | None in the text. |
| 7. Specific clusters | Gatos clusters that are doing fine. |
| 8. Voice | The narrator for the hub. A Gatos trader or dockmaster for the greeting: friendly, plain, proud of good work. |
